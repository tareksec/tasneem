What's Next & Simple Day-to-Day Operation

Your website is live, stable, and ready to welcome customers. Here is a brief, non-technical guide on how you can manage your site moving forward:

🧭

How to Run Your New Website Going Forward

Step 01

Checking Inbound Quote Requests

When a buyer submits a quote, it immediately arrives in your Admin Dashboard > Quotes pipeline and triggers a direct WhatsApp notification with their contact and machine details.

Step 02

Adding or Updating Machines

Got a new model from Taiwan or China? Log in to your Admin portal, click "Add Machine", type the specifications, upload a photo, and it is instantly published to your live catalog.

Step 03

Approving Customer Reviews

Whenever a satisfied client writes a review, you will find it waiting under Admin > Reviews. Click "Approve" with one tap to show it proudly on your homepage.
